# 🏁 Stage 5: Project Closure & Handover – TENTORA

> **TENTORA** is an AI-powered, curated creative-solutions platform that connects project owners with Ready Designs, visual taste discovery, and suitable creative professionals through one organized experience.

---

## 🎯 Overcoming Creative-Market Fragmentation

> Project owners often search across several disconnected platforms to find designs, understand creative services, compare portfolios, and manage projects.
>
> **TENTORA addresses this fragmentation** by organizing creative discovery according to the client’s sector and service, then providing two connected paths:
>
> - **Design Now:** Customize a Ready Design and reach a usable result quickly.
> - **Work With a Creative:** Discover visual taste, receive relevant recommendations, and manage the project until delivery.

TENTORA is not a generic freelance marketplace, unfiltered portfolio directory, or blank-canvas AI generator.

It is designed to shorten the journey from a creative need to a usable result while preserving human authorship, ownership, and professional execution.

## 🧭 Final Product Experience

### Shared Entry

`Sector → Service → Choose Product Path`

### 🎨 Design Now

`Sector → Service → Ready Design → Customize → Preview → Save or Export`

The client begins with a structured design rather than an empty canvas. Customization remains within creator-approved properties.

### ✨ Create with AI

`Describe Need → Interpret Request → Recommend Designs, Services, or Creatives`

The MVP AI experience focuses on discovery and controlled assistance rather than unrestricted design generation.

### 🤝 Work With a Creative

`Sector → Select Services → Taste Discovery → Main Taste Profile → Up to Six Recommendations → Selection → Workspace → Delivery`

### 🔄 Connected Handoff

`Saved Ready Design → Continue With a Creative → Transfer Design Context → Professional Development`

## 💡 Lessons Learned

Complete this section after the final retrospective.

### Product Lessons

- Did users understand the difference between the two paths?
- Were Ready Designs faster than beginning a custom project?
- Which editable properties caused confusion?
- Did one Main Taste Profile work across multiple services?
- Were six recommendations enough?
- Did users understand the recommendation reasons?
- Was the handoff to a creative useful and clear?

### Technical Lessons

- Which renderer worked best for Ready Designs?
- Which AI-assisted actions provided measurable value?
- Which features should remain rule-based?
- Was the modular-monolith architecture effective?
- Were the Taste Profile and Creative DNA structures flexible enough?
- Did progressive authentication preserve anonymous activity?
- Which integrations caused the most risk or delay?

### Team & Process Lessons

- Were responsibilities clear from the first sprint?
- Was QA involved before implementation?
- Were API contracts documented early?
- Was deployment tested early enough?
- Were creative rights reviewed during development?
- Was evidence collected continuously?
